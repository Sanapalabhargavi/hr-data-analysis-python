# 💡 HR Analytics Business Insights

## 📌 Overview

This document contains the key business insights obtained from the IBM HR Analytics employee dataset.

The insights focus on employee attrition, workforce characteristics, compensation, satisfaction, overtime, job roles, departments, and employee experience.

> Final numerical findings will be added after completing and validating the analysis.

---

## 📉 Attrition Insights

The analysis examines employee attrition patterns across different workforce characteristics.

Key areas include:

- Overall employee attrition rate
- Attrition by department
- Attrition by job role
- Attrition by overtime
- Attrition by business travel
- Attrition by job level

---

## 💰 Compensation Insights

The analysis examines employee compensation patterns based on:

- Monthly income
- Job role
- Job level
- Department
- Attrition status

These findings can help identify compensation patterns associated with employee retention.

---

## 😊 Employee Satisfaction Insights

The analysis evaluates:

- Job satisfaction
- Environment satisfaction
- Relationship satisfaction
- Work-life balance
- Job involvement

The objective is to understand whether employee satisfaction is associated with attrition.

---

## 📈 Experience & Tenure Insights

The project analyzes:

- Total working years
- Years at company
- Years in current role
- Years since last promotion
- Years with current manager

These metrics help understand how employee experience and tenure relate to workforce retention.

---

## 👥 Workforce Insights

The analysis also examines employee distribution across:

- Departments
- Job roles
- Job levels
- Education levels
- Age groups
- Marital status

---

## 🎯 HR Recommendations

Final recommendations will be based on the validated analysis results.

Potential areas include:

- Improving employee retention strategies
- Managing excessive overtime
- Supporting career development
- Reviewing compensation structures
- Improving employee satisfaction
- Strengthening workforce planning
