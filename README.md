# 👥 IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Workforce Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs HR data analysis using Python to explore employee attrition, compensation, job roles, employee satisfaction, experience, and workforce characteristics.

The goal is to transform employee data into meaningful insights that can help understand workforce patterns and identify factors associated with employee attrition.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Understand overall employee attrition
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition
- Analyze employee compensation and income patterns
- Explore employee satisfaction and work-life balance
- Study the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Analyze employee experience and tenure
- Identify workforce characteristics and patterns
- Generate data-driven HR insights and recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis identified several important factors associated with employee attrition:

- 📊 The overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- ⏰ Employees working overtime have a significantly higher attrition rate (**30.53%**) compared with employees who do not work overtime (**10.44%**).
- 🏢 Attrition varies across departments, with Sales showing higher attrition than Research & Development.
- 👔 Attrition differs considerably across job roles, with roles such as Sales Representatives and Laboratory Technicians showing relatively high attrition.
- 😊 Employees who left generally reported lower satisfaction scores than employees who stayed.
- 📈 Employees who left generally had fewer years at the company, fewer years in their current role, and fewer years with their current manager.
- 💰 Compensation varies substantially across job roles and job levels.
- 🎯 Workload management, employee satisfaction, career development, and early-tenure support are important areas for improving employee retention.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Reduce excessive overtime** by monitoring workload and staffing levels.
2. **Focus on high-attrition departments and job roles** with targeted retention strategies.
3. **Improve employee satisfaction** through regular feedback and workplace improvements.
4. **Strengthen career development** through training, mentoring, and internal opportunities.
5. **Support early-tenure employees** with stronger onboarding and mentoring programs.
6. **Review compensation structures** across job roles and experience levels.
7. **Improve manager-employee relationships** through effective communication and mentoring.
